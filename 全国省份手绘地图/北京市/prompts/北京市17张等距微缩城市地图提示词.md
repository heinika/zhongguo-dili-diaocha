# 北京市 16 区及总览：等距微缩城市地图提示词

## 使用方式

每次只生成一张图。复制“统一前置提示词”+ 对应地区的“专属内容块”+“统一结尾约束”，组成一条完整提示词。全套共 17 张：北京市总览 1 张、16 个区分图 16 张。

## 统一前置提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: illustrated isometric miniature-city map poster
Primary request: Create one high-resolution hand-painted isometric miniature-city map poster in a consistent Beijing series style. Use a warm ivory parchment background, a fine Chinese geometric border, a large accurate Chinese brush-calligraphy title, and a raised cutout base shaped like the real administrative boundary. Build a dense 30–40 degree bird's-eye miniature diorama on the base: small architectural models, terrain layers, rivers, lakes, parks, roads, railways, bridges, trees and local cultural objects. Use fine sepia ink outlines, restrained watercolor/gouache coloring, soft handcrafted shadows and a museum-quality miniature atlas feeling. It may feel three-dimensional and toy-like, but must remain an elegant hand-painted illustration rather than photorealistic CGI.
Resolution requirement: portrait 2:3, high-resolution final PNG, highest available resolution or HD export. Do not save only a low-resolution preview or screenshot.
Geographic rule: Use the real district map as the spatial skeleton. Preserve the administrative silhouette and the correct relative positions of major mountains, rivers, lakes, reservoirs, old city, new town, transport corridors and landmarks. Geography is more important than decorative density. Beijing is an inland city: never draw an ocean, coastline or island-like boundary.
Series layout: title and subtitle at the top; one large raised isometric district map occupying about 68% of the poster; six short plaque labels connected by thin lines; a small compass rose at upper right; six equal framed miniature vignette panels along the bottom occupying about 22%. Every vignette must contain a recognizable miniature scene, a short Chinese caption and one concise Chinese explanatory sentence.
```
