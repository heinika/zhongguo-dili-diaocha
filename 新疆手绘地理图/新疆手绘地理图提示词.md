# 新疆手绘地理图提示词

以下提示词适用于逐张生成一套统一的复古水彩地理海报。每张均采用真实的河流、山脉、盆地、绿洲、城镇与交通走廊关系作为构图骨架；不要画国界线或行政边界线。

## 统一风格尾注（追加到每一条提示词末尾）

`Style constraints: hand-drawn vintage atlas illustration, exquisite sepia pen linework with muted watercolor washes, weathered parchment texture, Chinese ornamental border, portrait travel poster, small compass rose, no photorealism, no flat vector, no modern UI, no watermark, no flags, no political boundary lines, no random illegible text. Chinese labels must be sparse, short, and legible.`
